## Why this blog exists

<!-- TODO: replace this with your own framing before the first article ships. -->

Most writing about AI sits at one of two extremes. Product posts describe what a system does without saying how it works or what it costs. Papers describe the mechanism and assume you already know why the problem is hard.

Neither helps when you have to ship the thing, keep it inside a latency budget, and explain the bill.

So I write the version I wanted: **each system worked out end to end, with the reasoning left in.** Not just the architecture, but the obvious approach, exactly where it fell over, and what the fix costs you.

## What you'll find here

Long, diagram-heavy walkthroughs. Numbers you can check. Interactive pieces where a slider explains something that prose cannot.
